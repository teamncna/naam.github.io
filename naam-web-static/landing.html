About NAAM

NAAM, the Nagarathars Association Address Manager, is a private community directory built to help verified members stay connected across Northern California. It brings member contact details, family information, native places, kovil details, and current locations into one secure and easy-to-use place.

More than just a list of contacts, NAAM is designed as an address book for the Nagarathar community. Our directory serves as a testament to our thriving community and a tool for strengthening the bonds that unite us all.
