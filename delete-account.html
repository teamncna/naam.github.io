Account & Data Deletion Request

App: NAAM — Nagarathar Address Book
Organization: Northern California Nagarathar Association (NCNA)

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request permanent removal of your account and personal profile data in either of two ways:

  • In the App (iOS, Android, or Web): Open the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap Request Profile & Account Removal.
  • By Email: Send an email to teamncna@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "NAAM App - Profile and Account Removal Request" and include your full name and NCNA Member ID (if known).

What Data Is Deleted

Upon verification, NCNA administrators remove your household profile from the active directory and revoke app access, including:

  • Primary and spouse names, email addresses, and phone numbers
  • Residential street address, city, and arrival year
  • Chettinad heritage details (Native Town, Kovil, Pirivu) and children's names
  • Uploaded household profile photo

Retention & Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. Once removed, your Google login email is de-listed and you will no longer have access to the NAAM directory.
